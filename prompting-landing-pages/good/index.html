FAQ

The questions we actually get asked.

Do we have to move our data into Pulse?

No. Pulse queries your warehouse directly with read-only credentials. Results are cached for speed, and you choose the cache window and region.

What happens when it gets an answer wrong?

Every answer shows the generated query. You can correct it inline, and the correction becomes part of your semantic model, so the same mistake isn't repeated.

Can we keep our existing dashboards?

Yes. Pulse is additive — most teams keep their BI tool for scheduled reporting and use Pulse for the questions that come up in between.

How long does onboarding take?

Median time to first useful answer is under three hours. Enterprise rollouts with SSO and residency requirements usually take two weeks.
